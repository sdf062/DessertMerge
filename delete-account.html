Account & Data Deletion — Dessert Merge

App: Dessert Merge · Developer: individual developer (contact: sdf062@gmail.com)

How to Delete Your Account and Data

You can delete your account and game data in either of the following ways:

  1. In the app: Open Settings in the game and use the Reset option. This deletes your game progress data.
  2. By email: Send a deletion request to sdf062@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in. We will delete your account data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What Is Deleted

  • Game progress and save data linked to your account
  • Your sign-in identifier (user ID) associated with the game

What May Be Retained

  • Purchase records are retained by Google Play in accordance with Google's policies (we do not store payment information).
  • Data collected by third-party advertising networks is governed by their own privacy policies.
